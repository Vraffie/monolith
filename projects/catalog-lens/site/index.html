See how an offer catalogue fits together

Open an offer-configuration package (a zip, or the unzipped folder) to get an interactive graph: offers → feature groups → charge clusters → bill types → tax, with up/downgrade rules, price timelines and consistency checks. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Drop a .zip anywhere on this page, or use the buttons above. Load sample opens a small fictional catalogue with a few deliberate mistakes.
